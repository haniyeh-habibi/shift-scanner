Reading the rota

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey
  2. Sign in with any Google account and tap Create API key
  3. Copy it and paste it below

It is your key, and it stays on this phone. Requests go straight from here to Google using your own free account. Nothing is routed through anyone else, and no key is ever sent to us.
The photo does leave your phone. It goes to Google to be read, and a rota shows your colleagues' names and hours as well as your own. On the free tier Google may use submitted data to improve their models. The offline reader never sends anything, but is much less accurate on these sheets.
